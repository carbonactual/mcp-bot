## 2. The accreditation ladder (cibng.org, verified structure)
CIBN accredits four categories under its Competency Framework:
1. Linkage Institutions (universities - MSc/ACIB routes)
2. Accredited Tuition Centres
3. Accredited Bank Academies (bank training schools - they get their own
   EXEMPTION POLICY, a real privilege)
4. Accredited Educational Training Service Providers (ETSPs)
Approval = official recognition 'for meeting set standards in training and
development', approved to deliver quality learning aligned with industry
requirements (per the Bank Academies page). Exact application forms live
behind JS-rendered pages / portal 'Download Forms' - obtain via
0700-DIAL-CIBN / cibn@cibng.org before applying. The current list of
accredited tuition centres is also JS-rendered (not extractable today).

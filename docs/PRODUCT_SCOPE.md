## Personalization model (the differentiator)
Every candidate gets a plan built from three inputs:
1. STAGE — not yet registered / student member / mid-programme / exam-countdown
2. COURSE MIX — their exact papers for the next diet
3. PACE — self-study sprint, steady weekly, intensive coaching
